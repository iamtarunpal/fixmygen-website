Annual Maintenance Contract

Planned service costs less than a breakdown.

An AMC puts your generator on a fixed maintenance schedule, so problems are found early and your backup power is ready when the grid fails.

Request an AMC Consultation Discuss on a call
Assessment
Site visit to note each generator’s capacity (kVA), running hours and condition.
Schedule
Visit frequency and scope agreed with you in writing.
Routine checks
Oil, filters, coolant, battery, belts, leaks and electrical connections.
Reporting
A service report after each visit: what was checked, replaced and due next.
Benefit
Fewer surprise breakdowns, longer engine life and a predictable yearly cost.
