Generator Servicing

Periodic servicing based on running hours.

Call +91 79820 84013 WhatsApp Us

Servicing your generator at the right running-hour intervals protects the engine and keeps fuel consumption in check. We service DG sets for commercial, industrial and residential sites.

A typical service includes

  • Engine oil and oil filter change
  • Fuel and air filter service
  • Cooling system check
  • Battery and charging system check
  • Inspection of belts, hoses and mountings
  • Load test and check of voltage and frequency
  • Service report with recommendations

Need servicing for several generators or regular visits? An annual maintenance contract (AMC) is usually the simplest option. Call us or send your generator details on WhatsApp and we will advise on the next step.
